# Partner API usage terms (integration draft)

- Approved catalog metadata and stock photos may be displayed in authenticated KeebVault owner dashboards with linked SwitchBook attribution.
- Metadata and images may be cached for up to 24 hours and served as last-known data during a temporary outage. Bulk or permanent catalog copying is prohibited.
- Public collection pages may not display SwitchBook-sourced photos or canonical specifications without a separate agreement.
- Force-curve availability may be shown only as the supplied SwitchesDB link. No force-curve dataset license is conveyed.
- KeebVault must honor lifecycle tombstones, corrections, image provenance, record revisions, and reasonable rate limits.
- Credentials and user tokens must remain server-side. SwitchBook may revoke compromised credentials or abusive clients.
- This draft must be approved by both parties before production credentials are issued.
